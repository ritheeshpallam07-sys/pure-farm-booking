# Pure Milk Direct

Create a simple, clean, premium-looking business website for an organic milk brand called:

“rp — raw & pure”

Business:
We provide fresh organic milk directly from cows to customers. The focus is on freshness, purity, natural quality, and direct delivery from farm to customer.

IMPORTANT:

Keep the website simple.

Do NOT build a complicated frontend or backend.

No customer login.

No admin dashboard.

No payment gateway.

No unnecessary features.

The main purpose of the website is to introduce the brand and allow customers to book milk.

Website structure:

HERO SECTION

Brand name: “rp — raw & pure”

Main headline: “Fresh. Pure. Straight from the Cow.”

Short text: “Fresh organic milk, sourced directly from cows and served fresh to your doorstep.”

Large primary button: “Book Milk”

Secondary button: “Learn More”

ABOUT SECTION
Title: “Pure milk. Nothing complicated.”
Explain that rp — raw & pure focuses on providing fresh milk directly from cows to customers, with an emphasis on natural freshness and quality.

WHY CHOOSE US
Create 3 or 4 simple cards:

Direct from cows

Fresh & natural

Quality focused

Delivered to customers

HOW IT WORKS
Show a simple 3-step process:

Milk is collected fresh

Quality is maintained

Customer receives the milk

BOOK MILK SECTION
This is the most important section.

Create a simple booking form with:

Customer Name

Phone Number

Address

Quantity of Milk

Preferred Delivery Time

Optional Message

“Book Milk” submit button

After submission, show a simple confirmation message:
“Thank you! Your milk booking has been received. We will contact you shortly.”

For now, do not create a complicated backend or database. Make the form functional on the frontend and clearly show where a backend/database can be connected later.

CONTACT SECTION
Show:

Brand: rp — raw & pure

Phone/WhatsApp placeholder

Address placeholder

Simple “Book Milk” button

DESIGN:

Minimal, premium, natural and trustworthy.

Use a clean white/cream background with subtle natural green accents.

Use large readable typography.

Use rounded cards and buttons.

Add tasteful farm/cow/milk imagery if available.

Make it fully responsive for mobile phones.

Keep animations subtle.

Avoid excessive sections and unnecessary design elements.

BRAND STYLE:
The brand name must always appear exactly as:
“rp — raw & pure”

Use lowercase for the brand name.

The website should feel like a small premium organic milk brand rather than a large corporate company.
