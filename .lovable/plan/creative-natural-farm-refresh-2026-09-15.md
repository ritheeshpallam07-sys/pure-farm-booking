# Creative Natural Farm Refresh

## Goal
Refine the existing `rp — raw & pure` homepage into a peaceful, trustworthy local-farm experience without rebuilding it or changing any booking and owner functionality.

## Visual direction
- Use warm cream and soft off-white surfaces with sage, natural green, earthy brown, and restrained dark-green accents.
- Keep the current realistic cow photography as the visual anchor, supplemented only where a focused farm or milk detail materially improves the page.
- Introduce subtle paper grain, hand-drawn leaf/grass marks, small dairy-inspired line details, gentle earthy shadows, and restrained curved section edges.
- Preserve generous whitespace and readable typography; decorative details will remain sparse and secondary to the content.
- Avoid bright colors, generic corporate styling, crowded decoration, oversized moving elements, animated backgrounds, and tech-like effects.

## Homepage updates
1. **Navigation and hero**
   - Keep the existing customer navigation and mobile menu.
   - Present the exact brand name `rp — raw & pure` clearly.
   - Use the supplied two-line heading, revised subtext, and a single `Book Fresh Milk` action.
   - Make the hero spacious and peaceful with stronger image composition and restrained handcrafted details.

2. **Farm-to-home journey**
   - Add a compact, elegant visual sequence directly below the hero:
     `From our cows → Fresh milk → To your home`.
   - Use simple cow, milk, and home line illustrations rather than large text or decorative emoji.

3. **Concise content sections**
   - Shorten About to the supplied heading and sentence.
   - Retain four minimal Why Us cards: From our cows, Fresh & natural, Carefully handled, Delivered to your home.
   - Simplify How It Works to `01 — We collect`, `02 — We prepare`, and `03 — We deliver`, supported by restrained farm line art.
   - Remove repetitive supporting copy while preserving the useful customer flow.

4. **Booking and contact**
   - Keep every booking field, validation, submission, stored-booking behavior, success state, and error state unchanged.
   - Visually integrate the form into the farm aesthetic and use the supplied heading and subtext.
   - Keep the contact placeholders rather than inventing details.
   - Keep the footer simple with the exact brand and supplied farm-to-home line.

## Motion and accessibility
- Add only gentle once-per-section fade-ins, tiny button/card hover changes, and existing smooth scrolling.
- Respect reduced-motion preferences and disable nonessential motion when requested by the device.
- Maintain clear focus states, semantic headings, readable contrast, image alt text, and touch-friendly mobile controls.

## Functionality safeguards
- Keep the existing database-backed customer booking flow untouched.
- Preserve private owner sign-in and stored-booking management at their existing unlisted addresses.
- Do not expose Owner Access in public navigation or content.
- Do not add maps, reviews, certifications, payments, accounts, invented claims, or new backend features.

## Verification
- Check desktop and mobile layouts for spacing, text fit, menu behavior, and visual balance.
- Submit a test booking and confirm the success message and stored-booking behavior still work.
- Confirm the private owner sign-in page remains accessible directly but unlisted publicly.
- Verify reduced-motion behavior and check for browser errors.
